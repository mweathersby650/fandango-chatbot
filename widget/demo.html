🎬 Fandango At Home

Movie Discovery Chatbot Demo

This page simulates the Fandango At Home browsing experience. Click the red film icon in the bottom-right corner to open the chatbot.

Try asking things like:

  • "A scary movie under $4"
  • "Family comedy in 4K"
  • "Sci-fi films from the 90s"
  • "Only show HD or better" (as a follow-up)
  • "Show me more"
Dev note: The widget is pointing at http://localhost:8080. Make sure the Spring Boot service is running with a valid ANTHROPIC_API_KEY.
